Privacy Policy

How ColorMeSilly! handles photos, payments, and your information.

App: ColorMeSilly! (com.colormesilly.app)
Owner: Sovereign Action LLC
Effective date: September 1, 2026

Short version: ColorMeSilly! has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no tracking. We do not sell your data. Photos stay on your device unless a grown-up math check succeeds and you ask us to turn a photo into line art. That photo is sent to fal.ai over TLS. We do not store it. Apple processes payments.

Who we are

ColorMeSilly! is an iOS app published by Sovereign Action LLC. It is listed in the App Store Photo & Video category. It is not a Kids category app.

Information we do not collect

ColorMeSilly! does not:

  • Show ads
  • Use analytics or tracking SDKs
  • Create user accounts
  • Collect names, email addresses, birthdates, or contact lists in the app
  • Use advertising identifiers
  • Sell, rent, or share personal data for advertising

Photos and line-art conversion

Photos you pick in ColorMeSilly! stay on your device by default.

A photo leaves the device only when you ask the app to make line art from it. Before that happens, ColorMeSilly! shows a short grown-up math check. If the check is not completed, the photo is not sent.

When the check succeeds and you continue, the photo is sent over TLS (HTTPS) to fal.ai, which runs the FLUX.1 Kontext model to create line art. The result is returned to your device. We do not store the photo.

fal.ai processes the request as our line-art provider. Their handling of data is described in their own privacy materials at fal.ai.

Data that stays on your device

Settings, coloring progress, and photos you keep in the app remain on your device. Uninstalling ColorMeSilly! removes locally stored app data.

Payments

Optional purchases are processed by Apple through your Apple ID:

  • ColorMeSilly! Plus: 7-day free trial, then $4.99 per month, auto-renewing until you cancel
  • Credit packs: $2.99 for 25 credits

Prices are shown in U.S. dollars and may vary by App Store storefront. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple’s handling of payment information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

To manage or cancel Plus, see Support.

Third-party services

  • fal.ai — line-art conversion (FLUX.1 Kontext) over TLS, only after the grown-up math check and your request
  • Apple — App Store distribution, StoreKit purchases, and optional system crash reports if you enable them on your device

ColorMeSilly! does not include advertising, analytics, or social-login SDKs.

We do not sell your data

Sovereign Action LLC does not sell your personal information and does not share it for cross-app advertising.

Children

ColorMeSilly! is a Photo & Video app, not a Kids category app. It does not require an account and does not ask for a child’s name, email, or birthdate. A grown-up math check appears before any photo is sent off the device for line-art conversion.
