How to cancel ColorMeSilly! Plus

Cancel in iOS Settings, not inside the ColorMeSilly! app:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your Apple ID name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Tap ColorMeSilly! Plus, then cancel.

If you cancel, you keep Plus until the end of the current paid period or trial. Unused time is not refunded by us. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

Manage purchases in the App Store

You can also review subscriptions and purchase history in the App Store: open the App Store app, tap your profile picture, then look for subscriptions or purchase history.
